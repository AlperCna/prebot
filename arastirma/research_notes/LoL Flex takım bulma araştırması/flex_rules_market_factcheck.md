# LoL Ranked Flex Rules, TR1 Market, and Fact-Check of Prior Claims (research date: 8 Nov 2026)

## How does Ranked Flex work today (party sizes, restrictions, own rank, value), and what changed 2023–2026 (incl. Clash and in-client teammate-finding)?

### Takeaway
Riot's current support article (dated 7 May 2026) says Flex allows parties of 1, 2, 3 or 5. A party of 4 cannot queue. Diamond and below have no rank restrictions, and anyone partying with a Master+ player must be at least Emerald. Flex has its own rank, separate from Solo/Duo. The biggest recent change is "/dev: Ranked 2026" (1 Dec 2025): Riot moved Flex MMR closer to Solo/Duo MMR, so that high Solo players stop stomping low Flex lobbies, and Flex never raises Solo rating. I found no Riot statement about Flex queue health and no official in-client LoL "party finder" for PC.

### Cited Findings
**Current rules (Riot official)**
- Flex party sizes: groups of 1, 2, 3 or 5 are allowed, and parties of 4 "must play unranked". Article dated 7 May 2026. — [Riot Support: Ranked Tiers, Divisions, and Queues](https://support.riotgames.com/league-of-legends/gameplay/ranked-tiers-divisions-and-queues) (old URL [support-leagueoflegends.riotgames.com/hc/articles/4406004330643](https://support-leagueoflegends.riotgames.com/hc/articles/4406004330643-Ranked-Tiers-Divisions-and-Queues) now redirects there)
- Flex rank restrictions: Riot says "there are no Flex rank restrictions for Diamond and below!" To party with Master or above you must be at least Emerald. Big rank gaps in a party may lead to "more difficult matchups". — [Riot Support](https://support.riotgames.com/league-of-legends/gameplay/ranked-tiers-divisions-and-queues)
- Flex has its own rank: "Your rank in Solo/Duo queue is totally independent of your rank in Flex." — [Riot Support](https://support.riotgames.com/league-of-legends/gameplay/ranked-tiers-divisions-and-queues)
- Solo/Duo, for contrast: queue solo or with one other person only. Duo partners must be within a tier window, roughly up to two tiers apart at Iron–Gold, narrower from Platinum to Diamond, and "within two divisions" at Diamond. Players with Master+ MMR face extra limits. KR players cannot duo with Master+. In the 7 May 2026 text, Grandmaster+ "must continue your journey alone". — [Riot Support](https://support.riotgames.com/league-of-legends/gameplay/ranked-tiers-divisions-and-queues)
  - CONFLICT: "/dev: Ranked 2026" (1 Dec 2025) says Solo/Duo duos return "at all ranks", including Challenger, "for most regions", because "recent boosting detection and matchmaking improvements" made it safe. The May 2026 support text still says GM+ is solo-only. Either the support page wasn't updated or the change didn't apply everywhere, and I couldn't tell which regions are excluded. — [Riot /dev: Ranked 2026](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-ranked-2026)
- The support article does not cover level or honor requirements. Clash's Summoner's Rift entry rules require level 30, ranked placements done in at least one SR queue this year or last, and SMS verification (VoIP numbers not accepted). This is per a third-party guide. — [lolnow.gg Clash 2026 (14 Jan 2026)](https://lolnow.gg/lol-clash-schedule/)

**Third-party descriptions of Flex (not Riot)**
- Dodge.gg (23 Mar 2026) repeats the 1/2/3/5 rule and says Riot disabled 4-player parties to protect solo players. It describes Flex before 2026 as often dismissed as a "joke queue" with "more variable" game quality. It says the Victorious skin can be earned by reaching Gold+ in either Solo/Duo or Flex, Flex decay is tracked separately, and full 5-stacks are preferentially matched against other 5-stacks, with an MMR offset otherwise. Only the 4-player ban and the 2026 MMR alignment are attributed to Riot. — [Dodge.gg Ranked Flex Guide 2026](https://www.dodge.gg/news/ranked-flex-guide-2026)

**History of changes**
- Patch 10.15 (announced 10 Jul 2020, released ~22 Jul 2020): Riot removed Flex party rank restrictions ("Queue with any of your friends, no matter their rank!", Mark "Scruffy" Yetter). Matchmaking was weighted toward the higher-skilled players, borrowing Clash's approach. — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/league-of-legends/riot-reveals-major-flex-queue-changes-coming-in-league-patch-10-15-1391185); [Win.gg](https://win.gg/news/5022/lol-patch-10-dot-15-removes-rank-restriction-from-flex-queue)
- 2023 (Riot, 6 Dec 2022): two ranked resets/splits a year, split points and a reward track, promos cut from Bo5 to Bo3, rank visibility removed from matches. Flex isn't mentioned specifically. — [Riot: Changes to Ranked in 2023](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/changes-to-ranked-in-2023/)
- 2024: three splits (Split 1 from 9 Jan, Split 2 in May, Split 3 in Sep), with reworked rewards. The Victorious skin threshold reportedly dropped to 1,000 split points. These are secondary reports. — [Tracker.gg](https://tracker.gg/lol/articles/riot-reveals-massive-ranked-changes-for-2024-three-ranked-splits-and-preseason-changes); [RiftFeed](https://riftfeed.gg/lol-news/lol-huge-ranked-changes-coming-soon)
- 2026 (Riot "/dev: Ranked 2026", Christina Zhao & Matthew Leung-Harrison, 1 Dec 2025):
  - "We're going to be bringing Flex ranks more in-line with Solo/Duo ranks." Riot's example of the problem is a player "who's Gold in Flex and Master in Solo/Duo".
  - "Flex will never pull Solo up, however."
  - Riot expects the change to "make Flex games feel a little more fair", while still letting people play with friends "(within reason.)"
  - The only queue-health line is a general one: "We'll be monitoring queue health, match quality, and your feedback closely."
  - Also announced: autofill and dodge changes, and shorter champ select timers in patch 26.1.
  - Source: [Riot /dev: Ranked 2026](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-ranked-2026)

**Clash**
- 2026 schedule (third-party, 14 Jan 2026): alternating SR and ARAM cups. Demacia (SR) 24–25 Jan, Ionia (ARAM) 21–22 Feb, Noxus (SR) 21–22 Mar, Ixtal (ARAM) 18–19 Apr, Zaun (SR) 9–10 May, Void (ARAM) 20–21 Jun, MSI Cup (SR) 18–19 Jul. Teams are 5-player premades in 8-team single-elimination brackets with a consolation bracket. Team tiers (I–IV) are set by members' MMR, weighted toward the highest-ranked player. Team formation opens on the Monday of Clash week, and the captain sets name, tag and logo. — [lolnow.gg](https://lolnow.gg/lol-clash-schedule/)
- Another guide says no Clash dates had been announced for Season 3 (starting 29 Jul 2026). — [1v9.gg Clash Schedule 2026](https://1v9.gg/blog/league-of-legends-lol-clash-schedule)

**In-client teammate finding**
- Wild Rift (mobile) has an official "Party Finder" with ranked and casual tags. — [Riot Support – Wild Rift Party Finder](https://support.riotgames.com/wild-rift/gameplay/party-finder)
- For LoL PC, the only official item I found is an old Riot post (Dec 2017) about one-click open parties and planned party voice. — [Nexus /dev: Upcoming party changes](https://nexus.leagueoflegends.com/en-au/2017/12/dev-upcoming-party-changes)
- Dotesports reported a "looking for friends" client feature with role, rank and Discord filters, but the article is undated and I could not verify it. — [Dotesports](https://dotesports.com/?p=441849)

### Inferences
- The 4-player ban means a 4-person friend group must either find a fifth or split up. That's a structural reason why "finding 1 player for flex" is a recurring need.
- The 2026 MMR alignment probably makes Flex ranks more meaningful and comparable to Solo/Duo, which could improve Flex's reputation. But I found no Riot data on Flex participation after the change.
- LoL PC has no official party finder of the kind Wild Rift has, which leaves room for third-party LFG tools.

### Gaps
- Riot's support text (GM+ solo-only) conflicts with the /dev post (duos at all ranks in "most regions"), and I couldn't establish which regions are excluded or whether TR is one of them.
- I found no official ranked requirement in this research (level 30 and a champion-count minimum are commonly cited, but I didn't verify them). The honor-level requirement for ranked or rewards is also not verified.
- I found no Riot statement about Flex queue health or population, and none about making Flex more attractive, apart from the 2026 MMR change.
- Clash on TR in 2025–2026: no source confirmed or denied that Clash runs on TR. Third-party schedules list no regional exceptions.
- I didn't verify the 2025–2026 split/season structure (number of ranked resets per year).
- I couldn't verify any official "party finder" or "find a group" test for LoL PC in 2024–2026.

## How big is the TR1 player base and its flex population, and what are its characteristics (demographics, play times, community programs)?

### Takeaway
On 8 Nov 2026, op.gg's TR tier page counted 886,635 ranked summoners, with the queue not labeled (probably Solo/Duo). Gold (22.6%), Silver (19.9%) and Platinum (18.5%) are the largest tiers. I found no TR-specific Flex population figure. Riot Turkey's own demographic statements are old: a 15–25 age range and a 19:00–22:00 peak from Feb 2020, and >22M accounts from Nov 2022. The amateur scene runs through official amateur tournaments under ESA, which has managed TCL since 2025, and through Riot's LoL Üniversite Ligi.

**Accounts and playtime (Riot Turkey statements)**
- Feb 2020 (Erdinç İyikul, Riot Games Türkiye Country Director):
  - 15 million accounts opened in Turkey in total, with more than 2 million registered in the past year.
  - 400 million hours of LoL played in Turkey in 2019.
  - Ages "15 – 25 arasında seyrediyor", with the player base "ağırlıklı olarak lise / üniversite öğrencileri".
  - Peak hours 19:00–22:00, and Saturday and Sunday are the busiest days.
  - Sources: [Fanatik, 14 Feb 2020](https://www.fanatik.com.tr/e-spor/riot-games-turkiye-direktoru-erdinc-iyikul-turk-oyuncusu-sonuc-odakli-2122318); [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/sporarena/turk-oyuncusu-sonuc-odakli-41448848)
- Nov 2022 (10th anniversary, İyikul): more than 22 million LoL accounts in Turkey, and LoL broadcasts watched for over 6 million hours in Turkey in 2021. — [Webtekno, 1 Nov 2022](https://www.webtekno.com/riot-games-turkiye-lol-hesap-sayisi-aciklama-h129358.html); [Mobidictum](https://mobidictum.com/tr/riot-games-turkiyedeki-onuncu-yasini-kutluyor/)
- The year before, İyikul reportedly cited more than 20 million accounts. — [Fanatik](https://www.fanatik.com.tr/e-spor/erdinc-iyikul-oyunlarimizi-bir-ayda-180-milyon-kisi-oynadi-2255690)

**Community and esports programs**
- Competitive structure: Turkey was folded into EMEA esports from 2023. The TCL champion plays in the EMEA Regional League pathway and EMEA Masters. This was a merger of competitive regions, not of servers. — [Webtekno, 18 Nov 2022](https://www.webtekno.com/league-of-legends-turkiye-avrupa-orta-dogu-birlesiyor-h129838.html)
- TCL management: ESA Esports & Media manages TCL for three years from 2025. The league keeps 8 teams with 3 seasons a year, and "Resmi bir ikinci lig düzenlenmeyecek". Official amateur tournaments run through the year as the path up to TCL. — [Webtekno, 30 Nov 2024](https://www.webtekno.com/league-of-legends-turkiye-sampiyonluk-ligi-degisiyor-h152671.html)
- TCL 2026 ran Kickoff, Spring and Summer splits, with Summer listed as 29 Jul – 28 Sep 2026. — [GosuGamers TCL 2026 Spring](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/tournaments/62847-turkish-championship-league-tcl-2026-spring); [rdy.gg TCL 2026 Summer](https://rdy.gg/es/lol/tournaments/117249)
- LoL Üniversite Ligi 2025 (Riot TR, 27 Mar 2025):
  - Teams are friends from the same university. Closed qualifiers need a Riot Kampüs Elçisi at the school, and the open qualifier doesn't.
  - Registration is on optimi.gg.
  - Qualifiers ran 7–8, 12 and 14–15 Apr 2025, sending 8 teams to playoffs (Bo3) and a Bo5 final.
  - Prizes: 1st place gets a University Esports Masters slot plus 40,000 TL, 2nd 20,000 TL, 3rd 10,000 TL.
  - Source: [Riot TR – LoL Üniversite Ligi 2025](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/tr-tr/news/league-of-legends-niversite-ligi-2025-balyor/)

### Inferences
- With ~887k ranked TR summoners, about 57% sit in Silver–Platinum. A Flex team-finding product would mostly serve mid-elo players.
- 22M+ accounts against ~0.9M ranked summoners suggests active ranked players are a small fraction of all accounts. Accounts include smurfs and inactive accounts, so 22M should never be read as a player count.
- The demographic and peak-hour data comes from 2020. The players who were 15–25 then are now about 21–31, so the age profile has probably shifted older. This is an inference, not data.
- With no official TCL second division, amateur 5-stacks need ways to find teammates for tournaments. This matches demand for Flex and Clash team-finding.

### Gaps
- No TR-specific Flex population or Flex/Solo share. op.gg doesn't label the queue, and LeagueOfGraphs and lolprofile blocked access.
- No Riot-published monthly active user count for TR. Third-party MAU estimates weren't found or verified.
- No post-2022 Riot Turkey demographic or play-time data was found.
- Not found: details of 2025–2026 ESA amateur tournaments (names, dates, team counts) and any 2026 edition of the University League.

## Fact-check of the six claims from the earlier analysis

### Takeaway
Claims 1 and 2 are confirmed. Claims 4, 5 and 6 match real Riot Turkey statements but are old (2020 and 2022), so they are "true but outdated". Claim 6 counts accounts, not players. Claim 3 is unverifiable: I found no site called "elwgraphs", and op.gg shows a similar but different TR total (886,635 on 8 Nov 2026).

### Cited Findings
1. **"Team Builder removed with patch 6.2 on 28 Jan 2016, folded into draft pick." CONFIRMED.**
   - The LoL Wiki says Team Builder launched 25 Mar 2014. Draft pick started taking on its elements (assigned roles) on 14 Jan 2016, and Team Builder was "permanently removed for all regions on January 28, 2016, with patch 6.2", replaced by the new Draft pick. — [LoL Wiki: Team Builder](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Team_Builder)
   - Season 2016 notes list "Removed: Team Builder" next to "New: Dynamic Queue". — [LoL Wiki: Season 2016](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Season_6)
2. **"Third Party Code API removed 7 March 2022." CONFIRMED (secondary).**
   - Hextechdocs says that, as noted in a Riot tweet, "the Third Party Code API has been removed… no longer usable as of the 7th of March 2022", and recommends RSO (Riot Sign-On) instead. I couldn't retrieve the Riot tweet itself. — [Hextechdocs (obsolete page)](https://hextechdocs.dev/using-third-party-code-to-verify-account-ownership/)
3. **"TR1 had 822,711 ranked Solo/Duo players as of 25 Aug 2026 (elwgraphs)." UNVERIFIABLE.**
   - A search for "elwgraphs" found no LoL stats site by that name. It may be a misspelling of LeagueOfGraphs, which blocked access. — [search context: LeagueOfGraphs](https://www.leagueofgraphs.com/rankings/rank-distribution/tr)
   - The closest available figure is op.gg's 886,635 TR ranked summoners on 8 Nov 2026, queue unlabeled. That's the same order of magnitude, so ~820k in late August is plausible, but I couldn't confirm the exact number, date or source. — [op.gg TR tiers](https://op.gg/lol/statistics/tiers?region=tr)
4. **"In Turkey, LoL is played most between 19:00–22:00." PARTIALLY TRUE / OUTDATED.**
   - Riot Games Türkiye's director said exactly this, adding that Saturday and Sunday are the busiest days, but the statement is from 14 Feb 2020. I found no newer data or methodology. — [Fanatik, 14 Feb 2020](https://www.fanatik.com.tr/e-spor/riot-games-turkiye-direktoru-erdinc-iyikul-turk-oyuncusu-sonuc-odakli-2122318); [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/sporarena/turk-oyuncusu-sonuc-odakli-41448848)
5. **"Turkish LoL player base mostly aged 15–25, mainly high school and university students." PARTIALLY TRUE / OUTDATED.**
   - The same Feb 2020 Riot TR statement says "15 – 25 arasında seyrediyor" and that the base is "ağırlıklı olarak lise / üniversite öğrencileri". It is now more than six years old and gives no methodology. — [Fanatik, 14 Feb 2020](https://www.fanatik.com.tr/e-spor/riot-games-turkiye-direktoru-erdinc-iyikul-turk-oyuncusu-sonuc-odakli-2122318)
6. **"Turkey has over 22 million LoL accounts created (webtekno)." CONFIRMED, with caveats.**
   - Webtekno (1 Nov 2022) reports İyikul's figure of "22 milyondan fazla hesap" at the 10th anniversary. — [Webtekno](https://www.webtekno.com/riot-games-turkiye-lol-hesap-sayisi-aciklama-h129358.html)
   - Caveats: this counts accounts, not active players, and the figure dates from Nov 2022. — [Mobidictum](https://mobidictum.com/tr/riot-games-turkiyedeki-onuncu-yasini-kutluyor/)

### Inferences
- In the final report, write claims 4–6 as "Riot Turkey said (2020/2022)…" rather than as current facts.
- For claim 3, swap in the op.gg figure with its date and the caveat that the queue isn't labeled.

### Gaps
- I couldn't access the original Riot tweet about the Third Party Code removal.
- I couldn't identify "elwgraphs", so I couldn't verify its 25 Aug 2026 snapshot.
