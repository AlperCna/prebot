# TR Player Voice: How Turkish LoL Players Find Teammates (pre / esnek / 5'li / "1 kişi lazım")

Research date: 2026-11-08. Scope note from the coordinator applied: the product idea is an on-demand "ad board" for when a friend group is short (for example 4 people need a 5th for Flex right now), so the notes put evidence about last-minute 5th-player search first.

Access limitations (important for interpreting "lack of evidence"): Technopat Sosyal, Ekşi Sözlük entry pages, top.gg, disboard.org, lolprofile.net, oyna.co and the ÇOMÜ thesis repository all returned HTTP 403/522 to the fetch tool. For those sources only search-result titles and snippets were available. Items based only on snippets are marked **[snippet-only]**. Every item is also marked **[anecdote]** or **[data]**.

## 1. What TR players say about finding a pre / last-minute 5th (forums, social)

### Takeaway
There is first-hand evidence of the exact "need 1 for flex" scenario on Turkish forums. The clearest example is a DonanımArşivi thread titled "LOL ESNEK 5. KİŞİ LAZIM" ("LoL flex, need a 5th person"), whose first post reads "Esnek giriyoz 1 kişi lazım" ("we're going into flex, need 1 person"). Within the same day it drew a rank/role qualifying question, a bronze volunteer (out of the requested range), and a moderator-style "go use premate.gg" reply. So the demand shows up as scattered, low-volume, ad-hoc forum posts, not as loud complaint threads. One structural fact makes the user's scenario common: Ranked Flex does not allow 4-person parties. A group of 4 friends must find a 5th, drop to 3, or not queue ranked.

### Cited Findings
**Last-minute "1 kişi lazım" posts (closest to the product scenario)**
- **[anecdote]** DonanımArşivi thread "LOL ESNEK 5. KİŞİ LAZIM". The full exchange happened on a single day (exact date not shown in the fetched text; the "online ders" reply suggests the 2020-2021 pandemic period, which is an inference):
  - First post: "Esnek giriyoz 1 kişi lazım"
  - Reply 1, pushing the poster to a dedicated tool: "dostum foruma her kişi böyle konu acsa ne olur?… premate.gg gir burdan bul" ("what if everyone opened a thread like this? go find one on premate.gg")
  - Reply 2, a timing mismatch: "online derste olmasam girerdm" ("I'd join if I weren't in online class")
  - Reply 3, qualifying questions: "Hangi lane rank?"
  - Reply 4, a volunteer below the range: "ben jungel bronzum ekle" ("I'm a bronze jungler, add me")
  - The poster then narrowed the ask: "rank gold -dia arası nick yazsın gelmek isteyen ekliyim" ("gold to diamond; post your nick and I'll add you")
  - In one short thread this shows three pain points: rank mismatch, timing/availability, and role being asked before anything else.
  - [DonanımArşivi](https://forum.donanimarsivi.com/konu/lol-esnek-5-kisi-lazim.1048052/)
- **[anecdote]** DonanımHaber "TR Server Flex e pre lazım", 02 Oct 2017, 23:29. The poster stated "Normal ligim Elmas 5" (D5 in normal queue). The first reply came 19 minutes later (23:48) and asked for flex rank ("şuan esnek ligin ne ?"). The next reply came about 37 hours later and was off-topic. 5 replies and 619 views in total, and no match was found in the thread. — [DonanımHaber](https://forum.donanimhaber.com/tr-server-flex-e-pre-lazim--129212783)
- **[anecdote]** DonanımHaber "Kendisine PRE arayanlar buraya!", 18 June 2019. This is a Discord server advertisement, not a request. It addresses flex grinders directly: "Tek/Çift ve Esnek ranked kasmak isteyenler kasamıyor musunuz?" ("solo/duo and flex grinders, can't you find a squad?"). It also advertises "Saygısızlık ve küfür kesinlikle yasaktır" (no disrespect or swearing) and its own bot. It got 2 replies and 696 views. — [DonanımHaber](https://forum.donanimhaber.com/kendisine-pre-arayanlar-buraya--138749892)

**Cultural signal: "pre" carries a negative reputation among solo players [anecdote]**
- DonanımHaber "Beyler nedir bu pre muhabbeti", 27 Aug 2014. Replies equate a "bot pre" with feeding:
  - "tr bot pre = feed"
  - "bot prelere asla güvenme" ("never trust bot pres")
  - Another reply said that in TR, someone insisting on "bot pre" is a feeder "%90" of the time.
  - This shows that TR players coordinate "pre" groups widely, and also that strangers view pre groups with suspicion.
  - [DonanımHaber](https://forum.donanimhaber.com/beyler-nedir-bu-pre-muhabbeti--96469029)

**Toxicity context (TR server) [anecdote]**
- A Technopat thread "LoL TR serveri vs EUW serveri" has a user arguing that racism and swearing are common on both servers [snippet-only]. — [Technopat](https://www.technopat.net/sosyal/konu/lol-tr-serveri-vs-euw-serveri.3006917/post-23387955)
- A Change.org petition links the TR server's small population to toxic players going unbanned. This is a user claim. — [Change.org](https://www.change.org/p/petition-to-change-riot-games-turkey-staff-automatic-chat-ban-system)

**Structural driver for the "4 friends need a 5th" scenario [data, rules]**
- Ranked Flex can be queued with groups of 1, 2, 3 or 5. Groups of 4 cannot enter ranked. Source: [Oyuneks blog](https://oyuneks.com/blog/lol-ranklari-siralama) [snippet-only].
- Another source says normal ranked allows only 1 friend, while Esnek allows up to 5. — [Atarita](https://www.atarita.com/league-of-legends-rank-siralamasi/)

### Inferences
- The recurring failure modes in the few visible threads are:
  - Rank mismatch: volunteers fall outside the requested range ("bronz" vs "gold-dia").
  - Timing: the person is available "but in class".
  - Role and rank asked before anything else.
  - Slow or absent forum replies: 19 minutes for the first reply, then 37 hours, with no match.
- Forums are too slow for a "right now" 5th, and forum regulars push such posts away ("what if everyone opened a thread like this?"). This supports a dedicated, fast channel.
- The 4-person flex ban turns "short one player" from a nice-to-have into a hard blocker for a 4-friend group. That is likely the strongest structural argument for the ad-board concept.
- Because "pre" groups have a bad reputation among solo players, a stranger joining an existing 4-stack may worry about being the outsider or scapegoat. Reputation and rating signals could matter for adoption.

### Gaps
- No Twitter/X, YouTube, Twitch or Reddit TR posts specifically about "pre bulma" or "1 kişi lazım" were found. r/LeagueOfLegendsTR could not be confirmed to exist or be active, and r/KGBTR (841k members) returned nothing relevant.
- Ekşi and Technopat post bodies could not be read (403), so complaint frequency cannot be quantified.
- No first-hand quotes describe no-shows after agreeing ("geldi sonra çıktı", "kabul etti gelmedi"). This complaint is plausible but unverified for TR.

## 2. Turkish LoL Discord communities: size, LFG structure, activity

### Takeaway
The biggest TR LoL Discord is the official "League of Legends Türkiye" server: about 138K members, about 21K online, and tagged LFG by Discord. It dwarfs the 19.5K "League of Legends TR" server found in the earlier report. A long tail of smaller TR LFG servers (1-25K members) also exists, including the streamer server of Dora "Stratera" Özsoy (about 25K). None of the fetched listings expose channel structures, LFG bots, response speeds or peak hours.

### Inferences
- The online ratio is about 15% on the official server (21K/138K) and about 18% on LoL TR. Even if only a small share of online members are looking for a group, a large live pool sits inside Discord at any moment. Any product probably needs to live in or integrate with Discord rather than compete with it.
- Mentions of giveaways ("skin/hesap çekilişi") and tournaments in small servers suggest LFG alone does not keep these servers alive. They bundle it with engagement hooks.

### Gaps
- Channel lists, role/elo selection roles, LFG bots and message volume could not be verified. Discord pages don't expose them, and seeing them requires joining.
- No data was found on how fast LFG posts in TR Discords get answered, or on hourly or peak activity in those channels. Measuring this needs a direct observation study: join the official server and log LFG posts and replies over a week.
- Streamer servers other than Stratera were not found (searches for other TR streamers failed).

## 3. How TR players currently form flex teams / find a 5th

### Takeaway
The visible channels are, in rough order of observed prominence:
1. Discord servers: the official TR server and community servers.
2. Forum threads: Technopat, DonanımHaber and DonanımArşivi, mostly ad-hoc and slow.
3. Classified-ad sites such as oyna.co.
4. International LFG sites such as premate.gg.
5. In-game friend adds and Clash.

No evidence was found for university e-sports clubs or WhatsApp groups as pre sources.

### Cited Findings
- **[anecdote]** A forum reply pushes "1 kişi lazım" posters to premate.gg. — [DonanımArşivi](https://forum.donanimarsivi.com/konu/lol-esnek-5-kisi-lazim.1048052/)
- **[data]** premate.gg describes itself as a free LFG platform for Valorant, LoL, CS2, PUBG Mobile and Brawl Stars. It offers duo and team finding, highlights Riot-verified players, and says it was founded in 2024 [snippet-only].
  - Semrush lists gankster.gg and zilliongamer.com as its competitors.
  - Note that the 2024 founding date conflicts with the DonanımArşivi reply that appears to be from 2020-21. The forum post may be newer, or the "founded" claim may refer to a relaunch. This is unresolved.
  - [premate.gg about](https://premate.gg/about-us), [Semrush](https://sv.semrush.com/website/premate.gg/competitors)
- **[anecdote]** oyna.co, a Turkish game-ad board, hosts LoL listings such as "LoL premade". Search snippets say they include "esnek gelecek birisi" requests with rank, city and Discord contact [snippet-only; site returned 522]. — [oyna.co LoL](https://oyna.co/ilan/oyun/league-of-legends-lol), [oyna.co listing](https://oyna.co/ilan/detay/lol-premade-esok)
- **[anecdote]** Technopat threads look for Clash teams ("LoL Clash için takım arkadaşı arayışı") and report trouble forming a Clash team [snippet-only]. — [Technopat](https://www.technopat.net/sosyal/konu/lol-clash-icin-takim-arkadasi-arayisi.3473087/), [Technopat](https://www.technopat.net/sosyal/konu/lol-clash-bir-takima-giremiyorum-veya-takim-olusturamiyorum.1967449/)
- **[anecdote]** One Technopat reply says the official LoL Discord servers make finding teammates easier [snippet-only]. — [Technopat](https://www.technopat.net/sosyal/konu/lol-takim-arkadasi.2093366/)

### Inferences
- An oyna.co-style Turkish classified board already exists, but it is generic and asynchronous (it collects contact details and then you add on Discord). The "right-now" element is the gap.
- Clash is a periodic spike in 5-person team demand, separate from everyday Flex.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found on school friends, university clubs, WhatsApp groups, or post-match "Arkadaş ekle" as sources of pre. These are plausible but undocumented.
- No survey data shows what share of TR flex players queue with strangers versus friends.

## 4. Turkish tools, bots and sites for pre finding

### Takeaway
Turkish-specific tooling is thin. "FindPre" appears to be a Discord bot listed on top.gg that offers duo/flex search with a registered LoL account, but it could not be verified directly (403) and a direct search found no other trace of it. Searches for "Prebul", "lolpre" and "duobul" found nothing. The active players are international bots and sites (premate.gg, Miko by Duoo.gg, Queue Up!, EZLFP), plus the Turkish classifieds site oyna.co.

### Cited Findings
- **[snippet-only, unverified]** "Add FindPre Discord Bot", top.gg bot ID 648805051359559690. A search summary says it lets you search for duo or esnek with your registered LoL account and DMs you the details when it finds a match. The page itself returned 403, and a direct "FindPre" search returned no other trace. Treat it as possibly defunct. — [top.gg FindPre](https://top.gg/bot/648805051359559690)
- **[data, snippet]** International LoL LFG bots:
  - Miko by Duoo.gg claims "over 20,000 Discord users" connected.
  - Queue Up! | Duo Finder has a vouch/report reputation system.
  - EZLFP offers AI duo matchmaking.
  - None are Turkish-specific.
  - [Miko](https://top.gg/bot/1096340606235717642), [Queue Up!](https://top.gg/it/bot/1446819621133877288)
- **[data]** premate.gg, founded 2024, LoL plus other games, Riot verification [snippet-only]. — [premate.gg FAQ](https://premate.gg/faq)
- No evidence was found that "Prebul", "lolpre" or "duobul" exist; none were found under these searches. (Mate4Game is covered by the earlier report and was not re-checked.)

### Inferences
- An on-demand "short one player, right now" board in Turkish, with TR-server-only scope and fast notification, seems unserved by any verified tool. The closest are generic duo bots and asynchronous ad boards.

### Gaps
- FindPre's status (server count, last update, language) is unverified.
- Turkish-made Discord LFG bots inside the big servers are unknown, because channel and bot lists are not visible without joining.

## 5. Flex player counts and TR demographics / play-time data

### Takeaway
There is no reliable current figure for TR Flex player counts. A LoLProfile TR statistics page reportedly shows Ranked Flex at about 16% of tracked games (unverified, snippet-only). The only official TR play-pattern data is from Riot Turkey in Feb 2020, now dated:
- 15M accounts
- Peak hours 19:00-22:00
- Weekends busiest
- Core age 15-25
- 400M hours played in 2019

The 2025 Gaming in Türkiye report puts the market at about $1.01B and about 50M players, but gives no LoL or PC-multiplayer breakdown in public summaries.

### Cited Findings
- **[data, snippet-only]** On LoLProfile's TR statistics page, Ranked Flex is about 16% of games played. The solo queue split is Platinum 23.5% and Emerald 20.8%, from "24,901 Ranked Entries". The update date is unknown and the page returned 403. — [LoLProfile TR](https://lolprofile.net/statistics/tr)
- **[data, official, dated]** Riot Games Türkiye Director Erdinç İyikul (Fanatik, 14 Feb 2020):
  - "Ülkemizde açılan toplam 15 milyon hesabıyla LoL", plus over 2M new registrations in the previous year
  - "Oyunun en çok oynandığı saat aralığı 19.00 – 22.00 aralığı"
  - "En çok oyun oynanan günler ise Cumartesi ve Pazar günleri"
  - Core age "15 – 25 arasında" (high school and university students)
  - "2019 yılında ülkemizde toplam 400 milyon saat LoL oynandı"
  - Turkish players are described as "hızlı sonuç almak isteyen"
  - [Fanatik](https://www.fanatik.com.tr/e-spor/riot-games-turkiye-direktoru-erdinc-iyikul-turk-oyuncusu-sonuc-odakli-2122318)
- **[data]** Gaming in Türkiye 2025 report (Mobidictum, 19 Feb 2026):
  - Market about $1.01B (+24.69% in USD, +51.46% in TRY)
  - Active players approaching 50M, 54% male and 46% female
  - Mobile $649M and 47M players
  - No LoL or PC multiplayer figures in the article
  - Full report: turkiyeoyunsektoruraporu.com
  - [Mobidictum](https://mobidictum.com/tr/turkiye-oyun-sektoru-raporu-2025/), [ShiftDelete](https://shiftdelete.net/turkiye-oyun-sektoru-1-milyar-dolar-sinirini-asti?amp=1)
- **[data, paywalled]** Statista has a series titled "League of Legends share of players on Turkish servers"; its content was not accessible. — [Statista](https://www.Statista.com/statistics/711552/league-of-legends-lol-share-of-players-on-turkish-servers/)
- **[data, global, dated]** An Upcomer article from early 2024, citing LeagueOfGraphs, gives a global Flex rank distribution: Silver 17%, Gold 16%, Platinum 19%. It is not TR-specific. — [Upcomer](https://upcomer.com/?p=161157)
- **[academic, inaccessible]** ÇOMÜ open-access item: "Çevrimiçi oyun oynayan bireylerin dijital sosyalleşmeye ilişkin görüşleri: League of Legends örneği" (views on digital socialization among LoL players). The page returned 403. A search snippet says LoL players can meet their need for socialization in the online game environment. — [ÇOMÜ Açık Erişim](https://acikerisim.comu.edu.tr/items/cf09eebc-b964-4e50-aaff-682bb85c1b7c)

### Inferences
- If Flex is about 16% of TR games and peak play is 19:00-22:00 on weekdays plus weekends, the "need a 5th now" moments likely cluster in the evening, which is also when the most potential fillers are online. This is an inference from 2020 Riot data plus unverified LoLProfile data.
- A core audience of 15-25-year-old students lines up with the "online derste olmasam girerdim" timing friction seen in the forum.

### Gaps
- No post-2022 Riot TR player count, Flex queue size, or Flex ladder size was found. op.gg, u.gg and leagueofgraphs Flex-TR distribution pages were not retrievable or not found.
- No TR survey covers how often players queue with strangers, or the reasons groups break up (no-shows, toxicity, mic, age).
- No peak-hour data exists specifically for LFG channels.
